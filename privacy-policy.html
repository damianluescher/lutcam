Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

LUT Cam collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your photos or anything about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera: used only to take your photos.
  • Photos: LUT Cam adds each developed photo, and its unprocessed RAW file, to your photo library. If you import a photo, only that photo is read.
  • Location: only if you turn on "Place in photos". The place is written into the photo file on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings: your look and preferences are stored on the phone.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. We receive no payment or personal details from Apple.
