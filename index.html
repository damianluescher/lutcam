How it works

A camera that leaves the photo alone.

No HDR, no sharpening, only light noise reduction when it gets dark. LUT Cam keeps the sensor's own data, develops it neutrally, then gives it a film look from a physical model of film and print.

  1. 01

    Shoot unprocessed

    A calm camera: lens, exposure, shutter. Every photo is a true RAW.

    SS · EV · ISO · DNG

  2. 02

    Pick a film

    Swipe through film looks built from real film science, not filters. The look is live in the viewfinder, so the photo looks like what you saw.

    8 films · Pure

  3. 03

    Change it later

    Every photo develops again from its RAW. Change the film, the strength or the grain any time.
