How it works

A camera that leaves the photo alone.

No HDR, no smoothing, no sharpening. LUT Cam keeps the sensor's own data, develops it neutrally, then gives it a film look from a physical model of film and print.

The RAW stays with you

Every photo keeps its unprocessed DNG beside it in your library.

Made for harsh light

On high-contrast days a film-like curve holds the sky and the shadows.

For colourists

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no ads, no tracking. No shot limits, no watermark.

Help

Where is the unprocessed file?

Every photo keeps its RAW (DNG) beside it in your photo library. In the app, open a photo and use Export RAW.

Can I change the look after the shot?

Yes. Open the photo and swipe through the looks. It develops again from the RAW, so nothing is lost.

How do I grade a photo myself?

Open a photo and use Export RAW. Next to the DNG you get an Apple Log file for DaVinci Resolve. You can switch that off in Settings, Export for Resolve.

I bought the unlock on another phone.

Settings, Restore purchase.

Which iPhones work?

LUT Cam needs a back camera that gives unprocessed RAW photos. iPhone 12 Pro or newer works best.
