Help

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

Your photos

Where is the unprocessed file?

Every photo keeps its RAW (DNG) beside it in your photo library. In the app, open a photo and use Export RAW.

Can I change the look after the shot?

Yes. Open the photo, choose Look and swipe sideways on the photo to change the film. It develops again from the RAW, so nothing is lost.

How do I grade a photo myself?

Open a photo and use Export RAW. Next to the DNG you get an Apple Log file for DaVinci Resolve. You can switch that off in Settings, Export for Resolve.

Purchase and phones

I bought the unlock on another phone.

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase.

Which iPhones work?

LUT Cam needs iOS 26, so iPhone 11 or newer, or iPhone SE (2nd generation) or newer. It shoots with the back lenses that give unprocessed RAW photos; if a lens can't, the app tells you on screen.

Privacy

LUT Cam collects nothing: no account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Read the privacy policy.
