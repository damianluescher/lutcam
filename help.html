Help

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

Your photos

Where is the unprocessed file?

Every photo keeps its RAW (DNG) beside it in your photo library. In the app, open a photo and use Export RAW.

Can I change the look after the shot?

Yes. Open the photo, choose Look and swipe sideways on the photo to change the film. It develops again from the RAW, so nothing is lost.

How do I grade a photo myself?

Open a photo and use Export RAW. Next to the DNG you get an Apple Log file for DaVinci Resolve. You can switch that off in Settings, Export for Resolve.

How do I fix the colour of the light?

Open the photo and choose White balance. You see your photo in six lights (As shot, Daylight, Cloudy, Shade, Tungsten, Flash): tap the one that looks right. Fine-tune with the Warmth slider; tap the word Warmth to switch it to Tint.

How do I see the welcome screens again?

Open Settings and tap Welcome screen. Your photos and settings stay as they are.

Free, Pro and phones

What is free?

Two looks, Pure and Portra 400, are free forever, with every lens, format and editor tool. No account, no watermark, no shot limit.

What does Pro add?

Every other film look. Pro is a yearly subscription that starts with one week free, or Lifetime, paid once. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy. The free week lets you shoot with every look before you pay.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the iPhone's Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then LUT Cam. Cancel at least a day before the free week or the year ends. Photos you took with a Pro look stay as they are.

I bought Pro on another phone.

Open Settings in LUT Cam and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Which iPhones work?

LUT Cam needs iOS 26, so iPhone 11 or newer, or iPhone SE (2nd generation) or newer. It shoots with the back lenses that give unprocessed RAW photos; if a lens can't, the app tells you on screen.
